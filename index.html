Our model

ICON's Place-Based Industrial Model identifies industries that different local economies could realistically expand or develop, estimates the employment they could support, and maps the disadvantaged neighbourhoods within each local labour market that should be central to realising those opportunities.

    Guide to the model

    Definitions

    Expansion

    Growth in an industry with a qualifying existing local employment base.

    Diversification

    Development of an industry that is currently absent locally but is supported by related industries and workforce evidence.

    Current Relative Comparative Advantage (RCA)

    Shows whether an industry accounts for a larger share of local employment than it does nationally. An RCA above 1 means the TTWA is relatively specialised in that industry.

    Industrial relatedness

    Shows whether industries commonly found alongside this industry are already strong in the local economy.

    Workforce suitability

    Shows how closely the occupations held by local workers match the occupations normally used by the industry.
